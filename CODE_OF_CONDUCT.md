# Code of Conduct

## Our principles

Otiyot Press is part of the Otiyot+ effort to make Hebrew learning more accessible. Our community seeks to act with **derech eretz** (דרך ארץ), **kavod habriyot** (כבוד הבריות), **emet** (אמת), **anavah** (ענווה), and **machloket l'shem shamayim** (מחלוקת לשם שמים).

We welcome good-faith participation from people who share the project's educational aims and are willing to collaborate respectfully.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with courtesy and respect.
- Critique ideas and work without attacking people.
- Be honest about sources, authorship, limitations, and mistakes.
- Give proper credit to Otiyot+, other contributors, educators, authors, and rights holders.
- Respect the accuracy and integrity of Hebrew and Jewish texts.
- Consider accessibility, privacy, and the needs of learners.
- Accept constructive feedback and engage in disagreement in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, bullying, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, persistent disruption, knowingly misrepresenting another person's work, sharing private information without permission, and deliberately sabotaging the project or its collaborative processes.

Strong disagreement, respectful criticism, and good-faith debate are welcome; they are not violations merely because they are direct or because others disagree.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing conduct that violates this Code. Depending on the circumstances, they may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or remove someone from the project.

To report a concern, open an issue if it is appropriate to do so publicly, or contact the maintainer privately through GitHub. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public report. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as circumstances allow.

This Code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other interactions carried out in the name of the project.
